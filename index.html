Avis is confirmed for a Suzuki Swift or similar: three-door automatic, five passengers, two bags and a full-to-full fuel policy.

The jetty allows essential personal items only and no prams, including compact folding models. Keep luggage concealed in the locked car and carry only a small day bag with valuables, medicines and electronics.
